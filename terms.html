2. What the app does

WakeUp is an alarm clock that requires you to complete a task — solving a problem, moving, or performing an exercise in front of the camera — before the alarm will stop.

3. Do not rely on this app alone

This is the most important term here, so it is not buried.

WakeUp schedules alarms through iOS. Whether an alarm actually rings depends on your device and on Apple's software: battery level, storage, Focus and Do Not Disturb settings, system updates, force-quitting the app, and bugs in iOS itself are all outside our control. No third-party alarm app can guarantee delivery.

If missing a wake-up would have serious consequences — a flight, an exam, a medical appointment, a work shift — set a second alarm using your device's built-in Clock app or a separate physical alarm clock.

We are not responsible for anything that follows from an alarm that did not ring, rang late, or was dismissed.

4. Exercise safely

The fitness missions ask you to perform push-ups, sit-ups, squats or jumping jacks, often within moments of waking.

  • You take part at your own risk.
  • If you have any injury, medical condition, or are pregnant, speak to a doctor before choosing these missions.
  • Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
  • Make sure you have clear space around you and stable footing.
  • The app counts repetitions. It does not check your form and is not a coach or a medical device.

Every mission can be exited without completing it — see section 6.
